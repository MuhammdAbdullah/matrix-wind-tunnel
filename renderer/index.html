Velocity Lookup Table

With the fan stopped, click Zero Sensor. Then click Start capture to run the fan at 10%, wait for the pressure to settle and type it in the Pressure box. Press Enter: the pressure is converted to air speed with V = √(2·|ΔP| / ρ) (ρ = 1.20 kg/m³, CP0704 Worksheet 1) and the fan moves on to the next 10%. Enter after 100% stops the fan. You can click any Fan % to jump to it, or type a velocity directly. Nothing is sent to the controller until you press Save Table.

Controller Network Settings

Offline

Written to the controller (V2.0.7). Needs a connected controller, Admin unlocked and no sweep running. The controller sends no reply to this command, so it is reported as sent, not acknowledged.

Controller defaults: DHCP on, port 1245. With DHCP the address is shown on the controller's touchscreen (Settings page); it cannot be read back over the API.

Connect To Controller Over Ethernet

The controller serves one Ethernet client at a time, so the first app to connect becomes the master and everyone else connects to it. If USB is plugged in (here or on another PC on this network), USB takes over.
